What to expect

Four focused weeks, everything done together, fast and in the open. Research runs the whole way through: surveys, customer interviews and user testing, so every decision is backed by evidence, not opinion.

  1. Week 1

    Evaluate the idea

    We pressure-test the idea with real market and customer research. Surveys and interviews tell us who it's for and whether they want it. You leave the week clear on the bet you're making.

  2. Week 2

    Brand and identity

    We shape your brand strategy, then your look and feel: logo, colours, type and a full style guide. The foundation you build everything on, and yours to keep from day one.

  3. Week 3

    Your web presence

    We design and build your website, landing page and CMS. By the end of the week you have a live site you control and can update yourself, ready to start capturing interest.

  4. Week 4

    Build and test the MVP

    We build your MVP app and put it in front of real users. User testing shows what to sharpen. You launch with a working product and real evidence behind it.
