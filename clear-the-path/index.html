As a business grows, the cracks show up quietly. Work that lives in one person's head. Spreadsheets held together with hope. A website that gets traffic but not customers. You feel the drag, but it's hard to see exactly where it comes from, or what to fix first. That's what the audit is for.

Two lenses, one clear picture.

We look at where you lose time inside the business, and where you lose customers online.

Operations & automation

We review your internal processes and interview your stakeholders to find the bottlenecks: the manual, repetitive and error-prone work. Then we pinpoint what's worth automating or digitising, and, just as importantly, what isn't.

Website & conversion

We run a QA and heuristic review of your website plus a conversion audit: where visitors drop off, what confuses them, and the changes most likely to turn traffic into customers.

How it works

A focused engagement, done with your team, not to them. Usually one to two weeks depending on size, with research running the whole way through.

  1. Step 1

    Discovery & interviews

    We sit down with the people who run the day to day. We learn how work really flows, where it snags, and what quietly costs you the most.

  2. Step 2

    Process & systems mapping

    We map your key processes and the tools behind them, and flag the bottlenecks, the double-handling and the manual work that's ripe for automation.

  3. Step 3

    Website & conversion diagnostic

    We audit your site for usability, quality and conversion, so your digital front door gets the same scrutiny as your back office.

  4. Step 4

    Prioritised roadmap

    We hand you a clear, ranked plan: quick wins, automation opportunities with rough ROI, and the bigger moves, so you know exactly where to start.

What you walk away with

  • A clear map of your processes and where they snag
  • The bottlenecks costing you time and money, named
  • Specific automation and digitisation opportunities, with rough ROI
  • A website audit: usability, quality and conversion fixes
  • A prioritised roadmap, ranked by impact and effort
  • A straight answer on where to move first

No fluff, no report you never read. Just what's slowing you down and what to do about it.
