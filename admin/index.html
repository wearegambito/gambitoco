Edit the copy on your campaign landing pages. Each field starts from the page's built-in text. Change it and hit Save to override, live instantly. Clear a field back to its original text to drop the override. Some fields accept simple HTML: <em> for the coral italic, <br> for a line break.

Content engine — ideas

Capture an idea and, optionally, drop in style reference images — Pinterest screenshots, saved Instagram posts, anything that sets the visual direction. References are described by AI on upload, then generation turns the idea + our style guide + those references into imagery and copy, and drafts the post straight into Buffer for review. Buffer is the approval surface — nothing publishes from here.
